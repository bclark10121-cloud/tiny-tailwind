# Tiny Tailwind v0.6.0

Play holding your phone upright or sideways. Back now sits at the top left of How to play and Wren's Wagon, clear of Android's bottom bar.

## Five minute test

1. Install the APK over v0.5.0. Your saves stay.
2. Hold the phone upright and open the game. The start screen fits the tall screen.
3. Tap Settings. Back sits at the top left. The list scrolls under it. Tap Back.
4. Play upright for a minute. The view keeps the same zoom, with more sea above and below. The dash button sits lower so your thumb reaches it.
5. Open the Menu. Upright, its buttons sit in two lines of four under the Menu button.
6. Visit Wren's Wagon. Back sits at the top left. With a controller, up from the first Buy reaches Back.
7. Turn the phone sideways mid-play. The game keeps going and the layout switches.

## New in this version

- Full play upright and sideways, on Android and in the browser. The game turns with your phone.
- Upright: the Menu row drops under the Menu button in two lines, and the dash button sits lower.
- Back at the top left of Settings, the slot list, How to play and Wren's Wagon. The Wagon's Close at the bottom is gone.
- Turning the phone mid-play keeps your game. Nothing reloads.

## Shared kit

Kit 1.13.0: the shared header bar with Back at the top and the screen fit for both orientations.

## Install

Version 0.6.0 installs over 0.5.0 and keeps your saves. The game follows your phone's rotation lock. Turn the lock off to play upright.

## Tested and not tested

Tested on the cloud test machine: the five-size browser check (phone and tablet both ways plus desktop, with a turn mid-play), the browser, controller, review and sound checks, the reach and bot runs, and the save, Settings, standards and Tester tools checks. The signed APK was checked for its signature, the tester gate and no internet permission.

Not tested: a real phone, a real tablet or the Retroid. The upright dash button height has not been tried by a thumb yet.
