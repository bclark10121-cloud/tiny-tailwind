# Tiny Tailwind v0.3.3

A closer camera and a calmer engine. Shared kit 1.5.0.

## New in this version

- The camera sits closer. The ship, enemies and the world all look 1.4 times bigger.
- In the warden's hall the camera eases back out, so the whole boss fight stays in view.
- Enemies no longer fire at you from off screen. With the closer camera, you always see who is shooting.
- Much less thrust from the back of the ship: a small flame and a light sprinkle of sparks.
- Everything else plays the same. Your save stays.

## Test checklist

1. Open the game page and press Play in your browser. The title card shows Version 0.3.3.
2. Start the game. The world looks bigger than in v0.3.2.
3. Fly around. The flame behind the Manta is small, with only a few sparks.
4. Approach a critter that shoots. It only fires once it is on screen.
5. Open Menu, then Map. The white box on the mini map is smaller, matching the closer view.
6. Enter the warden's hall. The camera eases back out for the fight, then closes in again after.
7. Android: install the APK over v0.3.2. Your save stays.
