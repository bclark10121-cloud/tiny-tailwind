# Tiny Tailwind v0.3.7

A new shot sound. The Manta's shots now make a sci-fi laser "pew" instead of a short click. Nothing else changed.

## Five minute test

1. Open the game page on a computer and press Play in the browser. Click once on the game.
2. Press Play and pick a slot. Hold fire. Each shot makes a laser pew that dives from high to low.
3. Keep holding fire for a few seconds. The pitch shifts a little from shot to shot, so it does not sound robotic.
4. Open Settings and set Sound to Off. The pew stops and the music keeps playing. Set Sound back to Full.
5. Install the APK over v0.3.6. The new shot sound plays on Android too.

## New in this version

- A new shot sound: Classic Pew, picked from six laser options. It is a square wave that sweeps from a high pitch to a low one in under a fifth of a second.
- Each shot plays at a slightly different pitch, up to 6 percent higher or lower.
- The shot sound keeps its old volume and still plays on every second shot, so fast firing does not wear on your ears.

## Shared kit

Built with kit 1.9.1. No kit change.

## Install

Version 0.3.7 installs over 0.3.6 and keeps your saves and settings.

## Full test checklist

1. Browser: holding fire plays the laser pew.
2. Browser: the pitch changes a little between shots.
3. Browser: Sound Off in Settings silences the pew. Sound Low makes it quieter.
4. Browser: the hit, coin, dash and other sounds sound the same as in v0.3.6.
5. Android: the laser pew plays when you fire.

## Not tested

- Real devices: no phone, tablet, Retroid or real Xbox controller was tried.
- Nobody listened by ear to the game build. A script in headless Chrome recorded each laser play and its pitch, and a second script measured the page's sound level.
- The APK was built, signed and checked, but never installed.
