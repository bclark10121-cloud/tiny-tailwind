# Tiny Tailwind v0.2.1

A new look for the ship. Shared kit 1.5.0.

## New in this version

- The default ship is now the Swallow glider: swept wings, a forked tail and a cockpit near the nose. Brian picked it from four shapes.
- The ship turns smoothly toward where you aim instead of snapping.
- The flame comes out of the tail and grows as you speed up.
- A new app icon with the glider.
- The ship's hit size did not change.

## Test checklist

1. Open the game page and press Play in your browser. The title card shows Version 0.2.1.
2. Fly and aim in a circle. The glider turns smoothly to face your aim.
3. Is the ship easy to see on the grass, in the cave and in the keep?
4. Android: install the APK over v0.2.0. The new icon shows on the home screen. Your save stays.

## Testing for this release

This is the 5th release, so it got the full end-to-end test gate under Brian's testing plan. Not tested on a real device.
