# Tiny Tailwind v0.3.9

The laser pew is easy to hear now. The music used to drown it out, so the shots got louder and the music got a little quieter. Nothing else changed.

## Five minute test

1. Open the game page on a computer and press Play in the browser. Click once on the game.
2. Press Play and pick a slot. Hold fire in the meadow. The laser pew stands out over the music.
3. Fly into Echo Hollow and hold fire again. The pew is still clear over the cave music.
4. Open Menu, then Settings. Set Music to Low or Off. The music drops or stops and the pew keeps playing. Set Music back to Full.
5. Install the APK over v0.3.8. The louder pew and the quieter music play on Android too.

## New in this version

- The laser pew is 8 dB louder. In the meadow and the cave it now plays about as loud as the music. Before, it was about 10 dB under it.
- The music is 4 dB quieter at every Music level in Settings.
- The pew still plays on every second shot and still shifts its pitch a little from shot to shot.

## Change or turn off the music

Open Menu, then Settings. Music has five levels: Off, Low, Medium, High and Full. Sound sets every other sound the same way. Your choice stays on the device for every save slot.

## Shared kit

Built with kit 1.9.1. No kit change.

## Install

Version 0.3.9 installs over 0.3.8 and keeps your saves and settings.

## Full test checklist

1. Browser: holding fire in the meadow plays a clear laser pew over the music.
2. Browser: the pew is clear over the cave music in Echo Hollow.
3. Browser: in the warden fight the pew is audible over the boss music, which is louder than the other tracks.
4. Browser: Settings, Music Off stops the music and the pew keeps playing.
5. Browser: Settings, Sound Off stops the pew and the music keeps playing.
6. Browser: the music feels a little quieter than in v0.3.8 at the same Music level.
7. Android: all of the above.

## Not tested

- Nobody listened by ear. The levels were measured: the page's sound output in headless Chrome, plus a mix of each music track against the pew at the game's own levels.
- Real devices: no phone, tablet, Retroid or real Xbox controller was tried.
- The APK was built, signed and checked, but never installed.
