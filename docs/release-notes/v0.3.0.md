# Tiny Tailwind v0.3.0

Release 2: coins and the shop. Shared kit 1.5.0.

## New in this version

- Coins. Enemies drop them. Trees can drop one the first time you shoot each tree on a visit. Coins fly to your ship.
- Breakables. Berry bushes in the Sunmeadow, crystals in Echo Hollow and clay pots in Thornkeep. Shoot them for coins and sometimes a heart. They grow back when you come back to an area.
- 10 hidden coin caches worth 122 coins in all. Some need the dash.
- Wren's Wagon, a shop in the first glade. Fly into the front of the wagon to open it. Seven items:
  - Repair kit, 10 coins: refill every heart.
  - Heart seed, 50 then 75: one more heart, two to buy.
  - Spare part, 30, 45 then 60: one more upgrade point, three to buy.
  - Coin magnet, 50: pulls coins and crystals from further away.
  - Shield charm, 75: blocks one hit and recharges at every checkpoint.
  - Quick dash, 80: the dash recharges a third faster. Needs the Dash Core.
  - Treasure map, 60: shows keepsakes and coin caches you have not found on the mini map and the Map page.
- A coin counter under the find counter. A shield icon next to it once you own the charm.
- The shop works with touch, mouse, keyboard and an Xbox controller. A or Enter buys. B or Esc closes.

## Test checklist

1. Open the game page and press Play in your browser. The title card shows Version 0.3.0.
2. Shoot an enemy and a berry bush. Coins fly to you and the counter goes up.
3. Shoot a tree. Sometimes a coin falls out.
4. Find the coin cache in the small nook below the first glade.
5. Fly into the front of the red and white wagon in the first glade. The shop opens and the game pauses.
6. Buy something with A or Enter. Close with B or Esc.
7. With the Shield charm, take a hit. The shield breaks first. It comes back at a checkpoint.
8. With the Treasure map, open Menu, then Map. Gold dots show keepsakes and squares show caches.
9. Android: install the APK over v0.2.1. Your save stays.
