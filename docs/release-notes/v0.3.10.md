# Tiny Tailwind v0.3.10

Sound effects work in the browser. Until now every sound effect was silent in the browser: the laser pew, hits, coins, the dash, level ups and the warden. Only the music played. Android was not affected.

## Five minute test

1. Open the game page on a computer and press Play in the browser. Click once on the game.
2. Press Play and pick a slot. Hold fire. You hear the laser pew over the music.
3. Shoot a bush and pick up the coins. You hear the bush break and the coin chime.
4. Open Menu, then Settings. Set Music to Off. The music stops and the pew keeps playing. Set Sound to Off. The pew stops. Set both back to Full.
5. Install the APK over v0.3.9. Sounds play on Android as before.

## New in this version

- Every sound effect now plays in the browser. The game's sound effects channel had no route to the speakers, and browsers drop a channel with no route. The music channel had one, so the music always played.
- The sound test now records what the page really plays while the ship fires, with the music cut, and fails if the pew is missing. It also checks that every sound channel has a route.

## Change or turn off the music

Open Menu, then Settings. Music and Sound each have five levels: Off, Low, Medium, High and Full. Your choice stays on the device for every save slot.

## Shared kit

Built with kit 1.9.1. No kit change.

## Install

Version 0.3.10 installs over 0.3.9 and keeps your saves and settings.

## Full test checklist

1. Browser: holding fire plays the laser pew, with a small pitch change from shot to shot.
2. Browser: hits on enemies, kills, coins, crystals, the dash, checkpoints and level ups all make their sounds.
3. Browser: Wren's Wagon plays its sounds when you buy something.
4. Browser: the warden fight plays its sounds over the boss music.
5. Browser: Music Off leaves the sound effects playing. Sound Off leaves the music playing.
6. Android: every sound plays as before.
