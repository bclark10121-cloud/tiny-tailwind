# Tiny Tailwind v0.3.4

Quality of life. Shared kit 1.5.0.

## New in this version

- Edge markers. Small arrows at the screen edge point to enemies off screen (red), Wren's Wagon (orange) and your next goal (gold star). The goal is the Dash Core, then the warden, then the nearest keepsake you have not found. Settings, Edge markers turns them off.
- Camera setting. Close (the default since v0.3.3) or Normal (the wider view from before). The warden's hall always uses the wider view.
- Fast travel. Every checkpoint you reach shows on the Map page. Pick one to fly there. It works outside fights.
- Find hints. When a keepsake is near, a soft chime plays and a gold ring grows from the ship. It does not show where. Settings, Find hints turns it off.
- Area counts. The Map page shows keepsakes and coin caches found in each area.
- Touch layout. Left-handed swaps the sticks and the Dash button. Fixed sticks keeps each stick in one spot instead of following your thumb.
- Color-safe shots. Enemy shots turn amber with a dark ring and a dark center, and your shots turn blue, so they differ by shape as well as color.
- Checkpoints have names: Glade, Crossroads, Riverbank, Far meadow, Cave mouth, Core chamber, Keep gate and Keep hall.

## Test checklist

1. Open the game page and press Play in your browser. The title card shows Version 0.3.4.
2. Fly toward the crossroads. Red arrows at the edge point to critters off screen. A gold star points toward the cave.
3. Open Menu, then Settings. Switch Camera to Normal and back to Close.
4. Reach two checkpoints. Open Menu, then Map. Both show under Fast travel. Pick one and you fly there.
5. Fly near a keepsake you have not found. A soft chime plays and a gold ring grows from the ship.
6. On the Map page, check the keepsake and cache counts for each area.
7. Turn on Shot colors: Color-safe. Enemy shots turn amber with a dark ring.
8. On a phone, turn on Touch layout: Left-handed. The move stick is on the right. Turn on Stick position: Fixed. The sticks stay put.
9. Android: install the APK over v0.3.3. Your save stays, and your current checkpoint is already in the fast travel list.
