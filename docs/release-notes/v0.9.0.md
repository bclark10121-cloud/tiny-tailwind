# Manta Quest: The Lost Pearl v0.9.0

Tiny Tailwind is now Manta Quest: The Lost Pearl. The whole game moves under the sea in the twilight lantern look you picked, with seven new foes made in Gemini and Meshy, gold and coral shots and The Lost Pearl story.

## Five minute test

1. Install the APK over v0.8.0. Your saves stay. The app is now called Manta Quest.
2. The title reads Manta Quest, The Lost Pearl.
3. Start a new slot. The Dragon King tells the story in the storybook box. Press Enter, A or tap to read on.
4. Fly the Lantern Reef. Look for the lantern jellyfish, the shrimp and the lionfish. Your shots are gold and coral. Theirs are violet.
5. On your phone, check you can spot every foe and every shot against the dark water.

## New in this version

- New name: Manta Quest: The Lost Pearl. The app id, the game page address and your saves stay the same.
- The twilight lantern sea. The Sunmeadow is now the Lantern Reef, Echo Hollow is Echo Grotto and Thornkeep is the Sunken Palace. Navy water, jade rock, coral, sea anemones, jade urns, glowing grotto crystals, red lacquer palace walls with gold trim, red lanterns and slow light shafts from the surface.
- Seven new foes, each from your lineup, each with its old moves: the lantern jellyfish (drifts after you), shrimp (swarm), lionfish spirit (aims), spiral clam (fires rings), pufferfish (charges), ink squid (fans shots) and Thornhull, the crab general.
- Shot colors: gold and coral for you, violet for foes. Color-safe shots in Settings are unchanged.
- The Lost Pearl story in the storybook talk box: the Dragon King's intro on a new slot and two lines from Thornhull. The end card now says you found the first shard.
- Keepsakes, areas and checkpoints renamed for the sea. The Warden Bell is now the Pearl Shard.

## Install

Version 0.9.0 installs over 0.8.0 and keeps your saves. Kit 1.18.0. Godot 4.7.2.

## Judgment calls

1. The folder, the GitHub repos, the package id and the APK file name keep tiny-tailwind, so links and saves keep working.
2. Foes are drawn from a high front view like the style board, not straight down. Straight down hid their faces.
3. The shrimp sprite comes from its Gemini picture. Its Meshy model came out as a flat slab.
4. Wren's Wagon keeps its name and look as a sunken cart.
5. The story skips in automated test runs so the tests keep playing.
6. The kit talk box sized itself too early on the first talk. The game measures it again after each page. A kit fix is proposed in the studio backlog. The arrow after "Press Enter for more" shows as an empty box in this font. That is the same kit fix.
7. The Game Shelf card keeps your 11/17 picture until you pick a new one (G2.7).
