# Tiny Tailwind v0.3.8

The graphics release. The meadow, the cave and the keep now glow, hits land with more punch and the menus use new fonts. Gameplay did not change.

## Five minute test

1. Open the game page on a computer and press Play in the browser. The title now reads Tiny Tailwind in a round, friendly font.
2. Press Play and pick a slot. Shots, pickups and the engine have a soft glow. The edges of the screen have a gentle dark vignette.
3. Shoot a bush and pick up the coins. A small +1 rises from the ship for each coin.
4. Shoot an enemy. A gold ring closes in just before it fires. When it goes down, a ring bursts out and the game holds still for a split second.
5. Open the Menu. The panel fades and grows in. Open Settings and turn Glow and color Off. The glow, the color grade and the vignette go away. Turn it back On.
6. Install the APK over v0.3.7. The new look shows on Android too.

## New in this version

- Glow: shots, pickups and the ship's engine glow, with a gentle bloom over the brightest parts.
- A color grade for each area: the Sunmeadow, Echo Hollow and Thornkeep each get their own tone.
- A soft vignette around the edges of the screen.
- Soft shadows from one sun under everything that stands or flies.
- Hit stop: the world holds still for a few frames on a kill, when you get hit and when the warden goes down.
- A camera zoom punch when the warden goes down. It follows Screen shake and Reduce motion.
- Enemy wind-ups: a gold ring closes in before an enemy fires. Aimed shots also show a faint aim line.
- A ring bursts out on every kill and on the warden.
- Glowing sparks and a glowing dash trail.
- Enemy health bars and the warden's bar show a pale part for the damage you just did.
- +1 and +2 numbers rise from pickups.
- The Menu and Wren's Wagon ease in.
- New fonts: Nunito for menus and text, Fredoka for the title and the labels in the world.
- A new setting, Glow and color, On by default. It turns off the glow, the color grade and the vignette.

## Shared kit

Built with kit 1.9.1. No kit change.

## Install

Version 0.3.8 installs over 0.3.7 and keeps your saves and settings.

## Full test checklist

1. Browser: the title card shows Tiny Tailwind in the new font.
2. Browser: shots, coins and the engine glow. The screen edges are a little darker.
3. Browser: the meadow, the cave and the keep each have their own color tone.
4. Browser: a gold ring closes in before an enemy fires. Aimed shots show a faint line first.
5. Browser: a kill bursts a ring, throws glowing sparks and holds the world still for a moment.
6. Browser: coins show +1, crystals show +1 and hearts show +2 as you pick them up.
7. Browser: the dash leaves a glowing trail.
8. Browser: enemy bars and the warden's bar show a pale part after each hit, which drains away.
9. Browser: the warden going down gives a ring, a hit stop and a small zoom punch.
10. Browser: Settings, Glow and color Off removes the glow, the grade and the vignette. On brings them back.
11. Browser: Reduce motion On stops the zoom punch along with the shake.
12. Browser: the Menu and the shop fade in.
13. Android: all of the above, and the game still runs smoothly on your phone or Retroid.
14. Xbox controller: every menu still works with the new fonts.

## Not tested

- Real devices: no phone, tablet, Retroid or real Xbox controller was tried.
- Frame rate on a phone with the glow on. If the game feels slow on a device, try Glow and color Off and tell BC.
- The APK was built, signed and checked, but never installed.
