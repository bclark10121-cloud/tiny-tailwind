# Tiny Tailwind v0.8.0

Release 3: weapons. Three new weapons to find, three Tuning Cores, a charged super shot and a score with your best. Finds rise from 20 to 26.

## Five minute test

1. Install the APK over v0.7.0. Your saves stay.
2. From the Glade, fly east toward the Crossroads. The Spread Shot sits in the hedge path. Fly over it. It equips itself.
3. Swap weapons: Y on the controller, Q or the mouse wheel on a keyboard, the Swap button on touch. The Ship page in the Menu lists them too.
4. Hold LT, E or the Charge button until the ring around the ship is full. Let go. A big bubble rolls through every foe in its way.
5. Calm a few foes in a row. The score under the coin counter climbs and a combo shows (x2, x3). Take a hit. The combo resets and the score stays.

## New in this version

- Spread Shot (Sunmeadow): three shots in a fan. Short reach, wide cover.
- Pierce Shot (Echo Hollow): slow, long and strong. Passes through a line of foes.
- Homing Shot (Thornkeep, past a rift): bubbles that curve toward the nearest foe.
- Three Tuning Cores, one per area. Each one makes every weapon better: more spread shots, more foes pierced, sharper homing, a quicker charge.
- Charged shot on its own button. Charging never stops normal fire.
- Score and best (G4.17). Points for calmed foes, finds and the warden. The combo grows by one every 10 foes in a row. A hit resets the combo only. "New best!" shows when you calm the warden. Best score, best combo and best on this device are on the Records page.
- Three new achievements: Full Quiver, Fine Tuned and Big Bubble.
- New Tester tools: each weapon on or off and Tuning 0 to 3.

## Test checklist

- [ ] Each weapon feels different and worth using.
- [ ] The charged shot feels strong and the ring reads clearly.
- [ ] Touch: Charge and Swap are easy to reach in both orientations.
- [ ] The score line is easy to read and not in the way.

## Judgment calls

1. Weapons are finds in the world, not shop items. One per area, so each area holds a new toy.
2. A new weapon equips itself when found.
3. Weapon picks: Spread, Pierce and Homing, as named in the roadmap. Their looks stay in the meadow style. The undersea redesign restyles them next.
4. Charged shot on LT, E and a touch button. It fires on release. A short tap does nothing.
5. Swap on Y, Q, the mouse wheel, a touch button and the Ship page.
6. The score run is the whole adventure up to the warden. A defeat never resets it (pillar 3, G4.1).
7. G4.18 completion and stars, G4.19 daily goals and G4.20 levels wait for a later release. They are open items.
8. Version 0.8.0, not 0.4.0. The roadmap kept 0.4.0 for weapons, but the game is already past it.
