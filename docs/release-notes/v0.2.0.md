# Tiny Tailwind v0.2.0

The Feel release: bigger shots and quality of life, from the first playtests. Shared kit 1.5.0.

## New in this version

- Bigger shots. Your shots are about half again as big, with a short trail. Enemy shots look about a third bigger. Their hit size did not change, so they are easier to see and no harder to dodge.
- Aim assist with four levels in Settings: Off, Light, Strong and Auto fire. Light starts on. It bends stick and touch aim toward the enemy nearest where you point. Mouse aim is never bent. Auto fire replaces the old Auto aim switch, and a save with Auto aim on moves to Auto fire.
- A mini map in the top right corner. It shows where you have been, checkpoints, doors, you and what the screen shows. Switch it off in Settings.
- Enemy health bars appear over an enemy for a moment after you hit it.
- A low health warning: the screen edges pulse red when one heart is left. A soft red flash when you take a hit.
- Auto pause. Switching away from the game, or a controller dropping out, opens the Menu.
- Screen shake setting: Full, Low or Off.
- Rumble for controllers, with an On or Off setting. It rumbles on a hit, a dash and the boss.
- Touch sticks setting: Small, Medium or Large.
- The Settings page scrolls, so every setting fits on small screens.
- The frame rate readout moved next to the Menu button.

## Test checklist

1. Open the game page and press Play in your browser. The title card shows Version 0.2.0.
2. Fly and shoot. Are your shots easy to follow now? Are enemy shots easy to see?
3. The mini map sits in the top right. Fly around and watch it fill in.
4. Hit an enemy. A small health bar shows over it.
5. Open Menu, then Settings. Try Aim assist on Light, Strong and Off with the right stick. Which feels best?
6. Try Screen shake Low and Off, and Rumble On with a controller.
7. On a phone, try Touch sticks Small and Large.
8. Drop to one heart. The screen edges pulse red.
9. Switch to another app or tab while playing. When you come back the Menu is open and the game is paused.
10. Android: install the APK over v0.1.2. Your save stays.

## Known limits

- Tested headless only. Not yet played on the ROG handheld, a phone or a real controller.
- Rumble was only checked as a request to the controller. Whether a browser passes rumble on depends on the browser.
