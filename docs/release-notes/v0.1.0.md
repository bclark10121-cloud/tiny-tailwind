# Tiny Tailwind v0.1.0

The first beta. One biome to test how the game feels. Shared kit 1.5.0.

## New in this version

- Fly and shoot with two thumbs, a controller, or keyboard and mouse.
- The Sunmeadow, the cave Echo Hollow and the keep Thornkeep.
- 6 enemy kinds and the warden Thornhull.
- The Dash Core. A dash crosses rifts and slips past shots.
- Crystals, levels and 4 ship stats: Power, Fire rate, Speed and Range.
- 20 finds with a journal. 3 of them add a heart.
- 8 checkpoints. Defeat sends you back to the last one with nothing lost.
- Assists in Menu, then Settings: difficulty, auto aim, game speed and invincible.
- A map page that fills in as you explore.

## Test checklist

1. Open the game page and press Play in your browser. The title card shows Version 0.1.0.
2. Press Start. Move and shoot. Does the ship feel quick and exact?
3. On a phone: left thumb moves, right thumb aims and fires. Do the sticks sit well under your thumbs?
4. With only an Xbox controller: A presses Start. Left stick moves, right stick aims and fires. A, RB or RT dashes once you have the Dash Core. View or Menu opens the Menu. D-pad moves, A chooses, B goes back.
5. Beat a few enemies. Crystals fly to you. At level 2, open Menu, then Ship, and spend the point.
6. Find the cave in the north-east of the meadow. Take the Dash Core. Dash across the rift inside the cave.
7. Cross the big rift in the east of the meadow. Enter the keep.
8. Clear the gated room. The gates open.
9. Get defeated once on purpose. You return to the last checkpoint with full hearts and everything you had.
10. Beat the warden. The end card shows your time and finds.
11. Open Menu, then Journal and Map. Do they match what you found and where you went?
12. Open Menu, then Settings. Try Relaxed, auto aim and game speed.
13. Close the game and open it again. Press Continue. Your progress is there.
14. Android: install the APK. The Back button opens the Menu.

## Known limits

- Tested headless only: a bot run, a browser run in headless Chrome and file checks. Not yet played on a real phone, with a real controller or with sound on.
- Difficulty is a first guess.
- One biome on purpose.
