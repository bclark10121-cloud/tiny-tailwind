# Tiny Tailwind v0.1.2

Smoother movement, from the first playtest on a ROG handheld. Shared kit 1.5.0.

## New in this version

- Smoother frames. The map is now drawn once and kept, so each frame only redraws the things that move. The game's own work per frame dropped from about 20 ms to under 2 ms in a slow test browser.
- The ship eases into and out of moves in about a tenth of a second, so a tap of the stick or D-pad no longer jolts it.
- The ship slides around the edge of a wall instead of stopping dead on a corner.
- The camera leads a little toward where you aim and travel, and eases so it never jumps.
- A Frame rate setting in Menu, then Settings. It shows the frames a second at the top right. Off unless you switch it on.

## Test checklist

1. On the handheld, keep Control Mode on Gamepad. Open the game page and press Play in your browser.
2. Open Menu, then Settings, and switch Frame rate on. Close the Menu.
3. Fly around the meadow. Does movement look smooth now? Note the frame rate it shows.
4. Tap the stick and the D-pad. The ship should start and stop quickly with no jolt.
5. Fly straight into the corner of a hedge. The ship should slide past the corner.
6. Fight a group of enemies and the warden. Does it stay smooth with many shots on screen?
7. Android: install the APK over v0.1.1. Your save stays.

## Known limits

- Tested headless only. Headless Chrome draws in software at about 6 frames a second for every build, so it cannot show real smoothness. The gain was measured as work per frame.
- Not yet tested on the ROG handheld, a phone or a real controller.
