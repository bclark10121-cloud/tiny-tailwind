# Tiny Tailwind v0.6.3

Nothing new to see. The shots, the enemy and warden attack patterns, screen shake, hit stop and the hurt flash now run on the new shared shooter core (kit K2). Tiny Tailwind is the first game on it. Sunset Static and later shooters will build on the same pieces.

## Five minute test

1. Install the APK over v0.6.2. Your saves stay.
2. Fly out and shoot a few critters. Shots, the glow and the hit pop look and sound as before.
3. Take a hit. The red flash and the shake look as before.
4. Use the debug menu to jump to the warden. Watch the rings, fans and spiral. At half health the warden bristles, calls four mites and fires faster, as before.
5. Calm the warden. Every enemy shot clears, the screen shakes and the camera punches in, as before.

## New in this version

- Kit K2, the shooter core (asked for by BC on 2026-11-19: "k2, shooter core next"). The game's shot pool, its four fire patterns (aimed, ring, fan and spiral), screen shake, hit stop, the hurt flash, the zoom punch and the warden's attack cycle moved into the shared kit. The game keeps its own rules for walls, bushes, trees and hits.
- Play is the same. A seeded bot run on Relaxed and Normal was traced every 20 frames (614 samples each) before and after. Every enemy, the ship, every hit and every pickup matched. One sample in each run differed by a thousandth of a pixel in a summed shot position: float rounding in the warden's fan and spiral speeds.

## Shared kit

Kit 1.16.0: new `shooter_core.gd` (Shots, Pattern, Feel, BossCycle) with its check `shooter_core_check.gd`. This release also takes the 1.15.0 kit edits that landed after v0.6.2: `title_y_upright` for the start screen title (not used here, so the title stays where it was) and `START_HERE.md` in the handoff zip.

## Install

Version 0.6.3 installs over 0.6.2 and keeps your saves.

## Test checklist

- [ ] Shots fly, glow and pop on enemies, bushes and trees.
- [ ] A hit flashes red and shakes the screen. Screen shake Off in Settings stops it.
- [ ] The warden fires rings, fans and a spiral, and turns faster at half health.
- [ ] Calming the warden clears its shots and punches the camera.

## Tested and not tested

Tested on the cloud test machine: the new kit check (`SHOOTER check ok`), the before and after bot trace, `COMMON`, `SECRETS`, `SAVESTORE`, `DEBUGMENU`, `SETTINGS_SLOTS` and `STANDARDS` checks, `REACH ok 20/20` with 10 of 10 caches, the browser test (59 checks, `errors []`), the controller test (`errors []`), `AUDIO ok` and `BUILD check ok` on the browser build and the signed APK.

Not tested: a real phone, the Retroid or a real controller. The landscape and portrait test did not run: no screen layout changed.
