# Tiny Tailwind v0.3.2

A new ship: the Manta. Shared kit 1.5.0.

## New in this version

- The Manta replaces the Swallow glider. It is a wide, rounded ray shape in sky blue with an orange trim band, a round cockpit and a glowing engine.
- It is 60 px across, a little bigger than the Swallow, and easier to see on grass and stone.
- It banks into turns and sideways moves, as before.
- Gameplay did not change. Speed, shots, the hitbox and your save are the same.

## How the Manta was made

1. Gemini drew four original ship concepts from straight above.
2. Each concept was placed on the meadow grass and the keep stone at real game size. BC picked the Manta.
3. Gemini drew a clean top-down Manta. Meshy turned that drawing into a 3D model.
4. The model was rendered into five sprites and tested in a game build next to a flat version. BC picked the 3D render.

## Test checklist

1. Open the game page and press Play in your browser. The title card shows Version 0.3.2.
2. Start the game. The ship is the blue and orange Manta with a dark outline.
3. Swing your aim in a circle. The ship turns smoothly and leans into the turn.
4. Fly sideways while aiming ahead. The ship leans the way you move.
5. Fly fast. The engine flame comes out of the tail.
6. Take a hit. The ship flashes red, then blinks.
7. Android: install the APK over v0.3.1. Your save stays.
