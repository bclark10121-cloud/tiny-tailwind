# Tiny Tailwind v0.3.1

The new ship. Shared kit 1.5.0.

## New in this version

- A new look for the Swallow glider. The ship is now an original 3D model, drawn from above: a blue body, cream swept wings with orange tips, a teal canopy and a glowing engine.
- The ship banks into turns and sideways moves.
- The engine flame, the hurt flash, the blink after a hit, the dash ring and the shield ring work as before.
- Gameplay did not change. Speed, shots, the hitbox and your save are the same.

## How the ship was made

1. Gemini drew reference views of the ship from the front, side, back, top and a 3/4 angle.
2. Meshy turned the front and side views into a 3D model.
3. A script renders the model from above into five sprites: level, and banked 15 and 30 degrees each way.

## Test checklist

1. Open the game page and press Play in your browser. The title card shows Version 0.3.1.
2. Start the game. The ship is the new blue and cream glider with a navy outline.
3. Swing your aim in a circle. The ship turns smoothly and leans into the turn.
4. Fly sideways while aiming ahead. The ship leans the way you move.
5. Take a hit. The ship flashes red, then blinks.
6. Dash. The dash ring shows around the ship.
7. Android: install the APK over v0.3.0. Your save stays.
